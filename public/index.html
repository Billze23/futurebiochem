Precision FAQ

Common technical inquiries regarding volumetric dosing and peptide compatibility.

How accurate is the volumetric calculation?

+
Our engine uses high-precision algorithms to account for syringe dead space and solvent displacement, providing accuracy within 0.01mL tolerances.

What is the shelf life of reconstituted peptides?

+
Typical research peptides remain stable for 30-60 days when refrigerated at 2-8°C after reconstitution. Always refer to specific documentation for each compound's reconstitution protocol.

Does the syringe size affect the actual dose?

+
The syringe size changes the visual resolution of the measurement (units). A 0.3ml syringe offers far more precise reading than a 1ml syringe for micro-dosing.
